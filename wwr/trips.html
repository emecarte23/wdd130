Our Featured Adventures

The Scenic Float

Perfect for families and beginners. This trip takes you through the calmest parts of the river, focusing on wildlife spotting and beautiful scenery. Includes a catered riverside snack.

The Stone Flow

Our most popular mid-range adventure! Tackle Class III rapids through the heart of the canyon. Expect plenty of splashes and high-energy paddling under the guidance of our pros.

The Thunder Run

For the adrenaline junkies. This Class IV-V trip is technical, fast, and exhilarating. Experience the "Thunder Drop" and navigate the most challenging sections of the river.
